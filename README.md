# Chime

A personal quest log set in a forest temple. Spill whatever's on your mind, and Mote, a small forest fairy, keeps watch. When something comes due she rings your phone like an incoming call.

## Install on the Pixel

1. On the phone, open this repository's **Releases** page and download the newest `Chime.apk`.
2. Tap it. Allow your browser to install unknown apps when Android asks.
3. Open Chime, press start, then go to **Options → Permissions** and turn all four on.

Every push to `main` builds a new `Chime.apk`. New builds install over the old one and keep your quests.

## How Mote reaches you

| When | What happens |
|---|---|
| A timed quest is due (minus the "Ring early" lead) | Full-screen incoming call with her ringtone |
| An all-day quest | Call at 9:00 that morning |
| An hour after a quest's time, still open | A "Psst! Over here!" nudge |
| Every so often between 9am and 9pm | A gentle check-in (Options → Mote's chatter) |

## Spill phrasing

`today`, `tonight`, `tomorrow morning`, `before noon`, `by 3pm`, `at 4:30`, `end of day`, `friday`, `next week`, `oct 15`, `11/3`, `in 2 hours`.
